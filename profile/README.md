# Pearly

Mint, trade and launch PRC-20 tokens on Pearl, with a wallet, swap and market in one place.

- Site: [pearly.fun](https://pearly.fun)
- Updates: [@pearlydotfun](https://x.com/pearlydotfun)
- The PRC-20 standard, its engines and the developer kit live in the [prc-20](https://github.com/prc-20) organization.

Pearly never messages you first. If someone claiming to be Pearly reaches out to you, it isn't us.
